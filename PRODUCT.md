# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Recruiters and HR professionals evaluating Pravin Kumar's background, skills, and work.
- LLMs, search engines, and other automated systems discovering and representing his professional identity.
- Friends, developers, tech professionals, and students learning from his technical writing and experience.
- Independent clients and organizations seeking help with projects, contracts, talks, or webinars.

## Product Purpose

pravindia.com is Pravin Kumar's personal knowledge hub and professional identity. It should be a clear, credible source of truth about who he is, what he does and knows, and how to connect or collaborate with him. It tells his evolving professional story, demonstrates his work, and shares what he learns.

## Positioning

The site brings Pravin's professional experience, projects, experiments, interests, and technical writing together in one personal source, rather than treating his identity as only a résumé or project showcase.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve detailed project case studies, technical blogs, experiments, learning notes, professional activities, and an up-to-date profile.
- Help visitors evaluate expertise and make contact about employment, freelance and contract work, technical talks, and webinars.
- Prioritize factual, verifiable content; distinguish professional experience from experiments and learning. Avoid exaggerated claims.
- Prioritize discoverability, SEO, and readability for both people and AI systems.
- Preserve simplicity, fast loading, accessibility, and easy navigation. Avoid a generic marketing-site treatment.

## Brand Commitments

The site represents Pravin Kumar as pravindia. Its voice should be personal, authentic, approachable, technically curious, and oriented toward sharing knowledge; avoid overly corporate language.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains project descriptions in `data/works.ts`, technical posts in `posts/`, and coding and typing activity dashboards. The README documents the sources and limitations of the activity data. Claims should remain grounded in real, verifiable evidence.

## Product Principles

1. Make Pravin's identity and expertise clear to people and machines.
2. Show credible evidence and distinguish completed work from exploration.
3. Share useful knowledge alongside professional accomplishments.
4. Make opportunities to connect and collaborate easy to find.
5. Keep the experience accessible, fast, and straightforward to navigate.
